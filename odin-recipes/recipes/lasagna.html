Chicken and Spinach Alfredo Lasagna Recipe



This easy white chicken lasagna with spinach is a different, yet delicious twist on a classic lasagna!.

Ingredients

  • 8oz package lasagna noodles
  • 3 cups heavy cream
  • 10 1/2oz condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 cups grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 large onion, diced
  • 4 cloves of garlic, sliced
  • 5 mushrooms, diced
  • 1 cooked rotisserie chicken, shredded
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 1 bunch of fresh spinach
  • 3 cups shredded mozzerella cheese

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add lasagna noodles and cook until al dente, about 8 to 10 minutes. Drain, and rinse with cold water.
  2. Mix heavy cream, cream of mushroom soup, Parmesan cheese, and butter in a saucepan over low heat; simmer, stirring frequently, until well blended.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook and stir onion in olive oil until tender, then add garlic and mushrooms.
  4. Mix in chicken, and cook until heated through. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Lightly coat the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish with enough of the cream sauce mixture to coat.
  6. Layer with 1/3 of the lasagna noodles, 1/2 cup ricotta, 1/2 of the spinach.
  7. Add 1/2 the chicken mixture, and 1 cup mozzarella. Top with 1/3 the cream sauce mixture, and repeat the layers.
  8. Place the remaining noodles on top, and spread with remaining sauce.
  9. Bake 1 hour in the preheated oven, or until brown and bubbly. Top with the remaining mozzarella, and continue baking until cheese is melted and lightly browned.
